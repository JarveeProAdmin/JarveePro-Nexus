## What Is JarveePro Nexus?

Traditional social media tools usually begin with one question:

> "When should I publish this post?"

Nexus starts with a bigger question:

> "What should my social media operation be doing today?"

JarveePro Nexus brings publishing, engagement, account management, AI tasks, and marketing workflows into one operating environment.

It is designed for people managing more than one account, more than one platform, and more than one campaign.

# Nexus Is More Than a Scheduler

A scheduler answers:

> "What should I post at 9:00 AM?"

Nexus is designed to help answer:

> "What should this account be doing today?"

That difference matters when you are managing dozens or hundreds of accounts.

A social media operation can involve:

**Accounts → Personas → Content → Publishing → Engagement → Timing → Targeting → Platforms → AI Tasks → Analytics**

Nexus brings these pieces into one operating environment.
